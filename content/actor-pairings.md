# Arcads Actor Pairings

This maps the script archetypes to the type of Arcads AI actor to select. Arcads has 300+ actors and the names rotate, so pick the closest match available in your account on the day. Always pick **AU/NZ accent** where the option exists; fall back to UK > neutral US.

## Archetypes

### `relatable_woman_late_20s`
- **Look:** mid-20s to early-30s, casual, approachable, not glossy
- **Tone:** warm, conversational, "telling a friend"
- **Use for:** social-proof hooks, accessibility hooks, reframe hooks
- **Scripts:** S02, S07
- **Avoid:** overly polished or model-coded actors — kills the relatability

### `tradie_male_30s`
- **Look:** late-20s to mid-30s male, casual, working-class energy (not literal high-vis)
- **Tone:** straight-talking, no-nonsense, "real talk"
- **Use for:** anti-cliché hooks, contrarian hooks, calculator/maths hooks
- **Scripts:** S05
- **Avoid:** suit-and-tie professional actors — wrong frame

### `mum_late_30s`
- **Look:** mid-to-late-30s woman, lived-in warmth, parent energy
- **Tone:** empathetic, slightly tired, hopeful
- **Use for:** family/kids angle, geo-targeted parent ads
- **Scripts:** S04, S10
- **Avoid:** glamour/fashion-coded actors

### `confident_woman_30s`
- **Look:** early-30s woman, professional but accessible (not corporate)
- **Tone:** clear, decisive, slightly authoritative
- **Use for:** maths/calculator hooks, time-pressure hooks, reframe-positive
- **Scripts:** S03, S09

### `mentor_male_50s`
- **Look:** 45–55 male, trustworthy, "broker uncle" energy
- **Tone:** measured, expert, never salesy
- **Use for:** authority hooks, rejection-recovery, hidden-info hooks
- **Scripts:** S01, S06, S08
- **Avoid:** anyone who looks like a politician or hard-sell salesman

### `young_male_early_20s`
- **Look:** early-20s, hopeful, slightly skeptical
- **Tone:** punchy, pattern-interrupt, "wait, what?"
- **Use for:** Gen Z / early-millennial pattern-interrupt hooks
- **Scripts:** (reserved for phase 2 hook variants H15, H19)

## Pairing rules

1. **Australian accent first** — even one obviously American voice tanks credibility on rent-to-own copy
2. **Two genders per hook minimum** in the test — you don't know yet which converts
3. **Match age to claim** — a 22-year-old saying "I help families every week" reads as fake. A 50-year-old saying "I'm 34, two kids, still renting" reads as worse than fake.
4. **Same script, different actors** is the highest-value A/B test in Arcads. Don't waste tokens on rewrites until you've tested actor variance.
