# Testing Matrix — Phase 1

## Token budget

- **Available:** 20,000 Arcads tokens
- **Approx cost per 30s video:** ~75 tokens (varies by plan, confirm in your dashboard)
- **Phase 1 spend cap:** 3,000 tokens (~40 videos)
- **Reserve for phase 2 winners:** 17,000 tokens (~225 videos)

The whole point: spend small to find winners, then spend big on the winners.

## Phase 1 — Wide A/B test

Pick the **top 5 hooks** from `hooks.csv` (mix categories — don't just take the 5 you personally like). For each, generate **3 actor variants** that match the suggested archetype.

That gives **5 hooks × 3 actors = 15 videos**. At ~75 tokens each, that's ~1,125 tokens.

Recommended phase 1 hooks (mix of pain points and styles):
- **H03** (stat / deposit-myth) → mentor_male_50s
- **H05** (story / Alan) → relatable_woman_late_20s **and** mentor_male_50s
- **H11** (calculator / opportunity-cost) → confident_woman_30s
- **H17** (emotional / family) → mum_late_30s
- **H08** (contrarian / bad-advice) → tradie_male_30s

That's 6 hooks × 2–3 actors ≈ 15 videos. Run them as paid ads (or organic) for 5–7 days each, ~$20/day minimum spend per video to get statistically meaningful read.

## Phase 1 — What to measure

Per video:
- **3-second view rate** (the hook's job)
- **Hold to 75%** (the body's job)
- **CTR on link** (the script + offer's job)
- **Cost per landing-page view** (the only metric that ties to revenue)

Don't kill a video on impressions alone. Wait for at least 1,000 impressions per variant.

## Phase 2 — Double down

Take the **top 2 hooks** by CTR and the **top 2 actors** by hold rate, then write 5 new script variants for each combination. That's another ~20 videos and meaningfully sharper.

## Phase 3 — Scale

Once you have a clear winner (one hook + actor combo with a CPL you'd happily pay all day), generate 30+ tonal variants — different opening line, different framing, same structure — to fight ad fatigue. This is where the bulk of the 17K reserve tokens go.

## Don't waste tokens on

- Rewriting body copy when you haven't tested actors
- More than 3 actors per hook in phase 1
- Long-form (>40s) videos until you've nailed a 25–30s winner
- Generating in non-9:16 formats before you've validated 9:16 (IG/TikTok/Reels)
