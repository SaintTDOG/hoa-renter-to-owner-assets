# Homeowner Assist — Brand & Content Guidelines

## Identity

- **Brand:** Homeowner Assist
- **Audience:** Australian renters aged 25–45 who feel priced out of home ownership
- **Core narrative:** From renting to owning — without the traditional 20% deposit wall
- **Anchor proof point:** Real client outcomes (e.g. Alan), not stock-photo testimonials

## Visual system

- **Palette:** deep navy `#1A1F2E` ground, antique gold `#C9A961` accents, warm cream `#F1E9D2` text
- **Typography:** condensed bold sans for headlines, clean sans for body
- **Imagery patterns:**
  - Before/after pairings (cramped urban vs. open natural; stress vs. calm)
  - Real client photos beat stock
  - Gold accents on dark grounds
- **Logo:** "H + house" mark in gold, "Homeowner Assist" wordmark stacked

## Voice

- Direct, no finance jargon
- Aspirational but grounded — never absolute
- Australian conversational (not Americanised); avoid forced "mate" / "g'day"
- Empathetic to rent-inflation pain without leaning into doom

## Compliance guardrails (Australian credit advertising)

We are not a credit licensee in this content. Treat all copy as informational and aspirational. The lawyer reviews the final cut, but the writing rule is:

**Never write:**
- "Guaranteed approval"
- "100% finance" / "No deposit ever needed"
- "No credit check"
- "Free money" / "Risk-free"
- "You will own a home" (definitive future promise)
- Specific interest rates or repayment figures unless legally cleared

**Prefer:**
- "See if you qualify"
- "There may be a pathway for renters"
- "Many renters are surprised to learn..."
- "Worth checking before you sign another lease"
- "We help eligible Australians..."

## Talent usage

- **Alan:** release on file (confirmed). His likeness may appear in static creative and as B-roll. Do **not** feed his photo into AI lipsync or face-swap tools without a separate written release covering AI use.
- **AI actors (Arcads):** generic voice talent, no implied endorsement of specific outcomes. Scripts must read as informational, not as a personal "I got approved" claim from the AI persona.
